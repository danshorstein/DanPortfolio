A little about me

I like the moment when “wouldn’t it be funny if…” becomes a thing you can actually use.

I’m Daniel: part numbers guy, part bass player, part weekend outdoors person, and full-time collector of rabbit holes. I’m happiest when a curious question turns into a prototype someone can poke at.

This is the evolving record of that tinkering—some practical, some ridiculous, all made with real affection for the details. Steal an idea. Remix a tool. Tell me what it wants to become.
